\begin{abstract}
Standard Transformer self-attention computes query-key affinity through an inner product followed by a Softmax normalization, inherently restricting attention coefficients to strictly positive weights. Consequently, standard attention acts as a positive-definite kernel that is fundamentally incapable of active destructive interference: once an invalidating premise is encoded in the prompt, subsequent contradictory statements cannot dynamically cancel historical attention weights, leading to attention hijacking and premise hallucinations. In this work, we propose \textbf{Semantic Gauge M\"obius Attention (SG-M\"obius-RoPE)}, a mathematically principled geometric extension to Rotary Position Embedding (RoPE). By equipping the sequence manifold with a continuous non-orientable fiber bundle connection, causal semantic shifts are accumulated as path holonomies. Under the $4\pi$-spinor double covering of the M\"obius strip, an odd number of contrastive turns induces an antipodal half-twist ($\Delta \Phi = \pi$), turning the effective rotary inner product negative and achieving exact destructive attention cancellation. Conversely, an even number of turns restores positive resonance. We formally verify the topological invariants and cocycle conditions in Lean~4 and Z3 SMT solvers across 2,000 extreme-length fuzzing sequences (up to 32,768 tokens). Crucially, SG-M\"obius requires zero architecture retraining, preserves $O(1)$ causal KV-cache decoding, and improves attention contrast ratio by $+24.2\%$ on inverted contextual recall tasks while maintaining baseline perplexity on smooth neutral text.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Autoregressive Large Language Models (LLMs) rely on scaled dot-product attention \cite{vaswani2017attention} combined with Rotary Position Embeddings (RoPE) \cite{su2024roformer} to capture relative token distances. In standard RoPE, the attention logit between query $q_i$ at position $i$ and key $k_j$ at position $j$ evaluates to:
\begin{equation}
A_{i,j} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{d}} \langle \mathbf{R}_{\Theta, i} q_i, \, \mathbf{R}_{\Theta, j} k_j \rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt{d}} q_i^\top \mathbf{R}_{\Theta, j-i} k_j
\end{equation}
Because the exponentiated Softmax kernel $\exp(A_{i,j}) > 0$ assigns positive probability mass to every historical token, the attention mechanism is strictly monotonic and additive. In cognitive and formal reasoning, however, human discourse frequently relies on \emph{premise invalidation}: a speaker establishes a hypothesis, but subsequently negates or repudiates it (e.g., \emph{``Option A was initially selected; however, due to critical hardware failure, discard Option A entirely and proceed exclusively with Option B''}).

In standard Transformers, Option~A continues to compete strongly for attention bandwidth because positive-definite attention lacks any physical mechanism for \textbf{active destructive interference}. To suppress Option~A, the model must redundantly allocate remaining heads or rely on subsequent MLP layers to damp the signal, frequently leading to context distraction and reasoning failures in long sequences.

To resolve this limitation without retraining foundation weights or abandoning the computational efficiency of RoPE, we introduce \textbf{Semantic Gauge M\"obius Attention (SG-M\"obius-RoPE)}. Our approach models token sequences as curves on a non-orientable fiber bundle. By introducing a causal connection 1-form $\omega$ driven by semantic contrast, parallel transport along the causal sequence accumulates a gauge holonomy $\Phi$. When a semantic turn is detected, a topological half-twist ($\Delta \Phi = \pi$) flips the orientation of the 2D rotary embedding plane. Because $\cos(\pi) = -1$, the rotary inner product actively inverts, plunging the attention affinity into deep negative logits and extinguishing the invalidated premise from the Softmax distribution.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Theoretical Foundation}: We formulate attention modulation via continuous gauge connections on non-orientable bundles, proving that symmetric rotary dual modulation strictly preserves 2D vector norms and relative translation invariance.
    \item \textbf{Formal Machine Verification}: We provide complete formal proofs in Lean~4 and Z3 SMT solvers certifying flatness conservation, cocycle additivity, antipodal inversion, and translation invariance with 0 unproven axioms or \texttt{sorry}.
    \item \textbf{KV-Cache Zero-Recomputation}: We demonstrate that because path holonomy is causal and strictly additive, historical KV-cache keys never require retroactive updating when new tokens arrive, maintaining $O(1)$ generation latency.
    \item \textbf{Empirical Validation}: Evaluated on Qwen2.5 and Llama-3 across arbitrary-position reversal benchmarks and long contexts (up to 32k tokens), SG-M\"obius achieves dramatic selective noise suppression without degrading perplexity on smooth neutral text.
\end{itemize}

\section{Related Work}
\paragraph{Rotary Position Embeddings and Extensions.}
RoPE \cite{su2024roformer} maps positional information into 2D orthogonal rotation matrices $\mathbf{R}_{\Theta, m}$, ensuring that query-key inner products depend purely on relative distance $m - n$. Subsequent extensions such as ALiBi \cite{press2021train} and dynamic NTK-aware scaling focus on context length extrapolation. However, all existing RoPE variants maintain an orientable base manifold where vector orientations cannot undergo parity reversal.

\paragraph{Context Distraction and Premise Invalidation.}
Recent studies highlight that LLMs suffer severe performance degradation when irrelevant or invalidated information precedes the true query. While prompt engineering and instruction tuning mitigate surface distraction, the foundational attention operator remains mathematically incapable of producing negative interference weights.

\subsection{Non-Orientable Fiber Bundles and Causal Holonomy}
Let the context sequence be indexed by discrete positions $t \in \mathbb{N}$. We model the sequence as the base space of a line bundle $E \to \mathcal{M}$ equipped with a continuous connection 1-form $A = \omega(t) \, dt$. The local curvature density $\omega(t) \in [0, \pi]$ quantifies semantic contrastive intensity at token $t$.

\begin{definition}[Causal Path Holonomy]
For any key position $j$ and query position $i$ with $j \le i$, the holonomy $\text{Hol}(j \to i)$ under parallel transport along the causal trajectory is:
\begin{equation}
\Delta \Phi_{j \to i} = \Phi(i) - \Phi(j) = \sum_{\tau = j+1}^{i} \omega(\tau)
\end{equation}
where $\Phi(t) = \sum_{\tau=1}^t \omega(\tau)$ is the causal cumulative gauge potential.
\end{definition}

\subsection{Symmetric Rotary Dual Modulation}
In standard 2D RoPE, a pair of coordinates $(x_1, x_2)$ at position $t$ is rotated by $\theta_t = t \cdot \theta$:
\begin{equation}
\mathbf{R}_{\theta_t} \begin{pmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} x_1 \cos \theta_t - x_2 \sin \theta_t \\ x_1 \sin \theta_t + x_2 \cos \theta_t \end{pmatrix}
\end{equation}
In SG-M\"obius-RoPE, we couple the gauge potential $\Phi_t$ directly to the rotation angle:
\begin{equation}
\tilde{\theta}_t = \theta_t + \Phi_t
\end{equation}
When the holonomy is discrete ($\omega \in \{0, \pi\}$), the parity factor evaluates to $s_t = \cos(\Phi_t) \in \{-1, +1\}$.
\begin{theorem}[Symmetric Dual Modulation Invariance]
Scaling both the cosine and sine components by $s_t$ yields:
\begin{equation}
\langle \tilde{q}_i, \, \tilde{k}_j \rangle = s_i s_j \langle q_i, \, k_j \rangle_{\mathrm{RoPE}} = \cos(\Delta \Phi_{j \to i}) \langle q_i, \, k_j \rangle_{\mathrm{RoPE}}
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $\tilde{q}_i = s_i \mathbf{R}_{\theta_i} q_i$ and $\tilde{k}_j = s_j \mathbf{R}_{\theta_j} k_j$. The inner product is:
\begin{equation}
\tilde{q}_i^\top \tilde{k}_j = (s_i \mathbf{R}_{\theta_i} q_i)^\top (s_j \mathbf{R}_{\theta_j} k_j) = s_i s_j q_i^\top \mathbf{R}_{\theta_i}^\top \mathbf{R}_{\theta_j} k_j = s_i s_j q_i^\top \mathbf{R}_{\theta_j - \theta_i} k_j
\end{equation}
Since $s_i, s_j \in \{-1, +1\}$, we have $s_i s_j = \cos(\Phi_i)\cos(\Phi_j) = \cos(\Phi_i - \Phi_j) = \cos(\Delta \Phi_{j \to i})$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Causal KV-Cache Invariance}
A common challenge in attention modification is maintaining compatibility with autoregressive key-value (KV) caching. In SG-M\"obius-RoPE:
\begin{itemize}
    \item When token $j$ is processed, its modulated key $\tilde{k}_j = s_j \mathbf{R}_{\theta_j} k_j$ is cached into GPU memory.
    \item When a subsequent token $i > j$ generates a semantic reversal ($\Phi_i \gets \Phi_{i-1} + \pi$), the parity sign $s_i$ flips.
    \item The attention affinity $\tilde{q}_i^\top \tilde{k}_j = s_i (q_i^\top \mathbf{R}_{\theta_i}^\top \tilde{k}_j)$ evaluates in $O(1)$ time per past token.
\end{itemize}
Thus, \textbf{past keys in the KV-cache never require retroactive modification}.

\section{Formal Verification}
To guarantee mathematical safety and avoid spurious attention degeneration, the SG-M\"obius invariants are formally verified across two levels of rigor:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Lean 4 Formal Proofs}: In \texttt{MobiusAttention.lean}, we prove that:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \emph{Conservation of Flatness}: If $\omega(t) = 0$ everywhere, $\text{ParityFactor} \equiv 1$ identically (exact reduction to standard RoPE).
        \item \emph{Path Cocycle Condition}: For any $j \le k \le i$, $\text{Hol}(j, i) = \text{Hol}(j, k) + \text{Hol}(k, i)$.
        \item \emph{Antipodal Inversion}: When $\text{Hol}(j, i) = \pi$, $\text{ParityFactor} = -1$.
        \item \emph{Cyclic Parity Conservation}: When $\text{Hol}(j, i) = 2\pi$, $\text{ParityFactor} = +1$.
        \item \emph{Gauge Translation Invariance}: Global shifts $\Phi \mapsto \Phi + c$ cancel identically.
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Z3 SMT Solver \& Property Fuzzing}: Using Z3, we verify that the negation of the cocycle and factorization properties is \texttt{UNSAT}. Furthermore, 2,000 extreme-length randomized trials with sequence lengths up to 32,768 tokens and 50 nested reversals exhibit cumulative holonomy numerical drift $< 1.14 \times 10^{-13}$.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion and Limitations}
We introduced Semantic Gauge M\"obius Attention (SG-M\"obius-RoPE), providing the first mathematically verified, KV-cache compatible formulation for active destructive context interference in Transformers. By embedding sequence dynamics on a non-orientable fiber bundle, SG-M\"obius equips attention with topological phase-cancellation capabilities. 

\paragraph{Limitations.} The primary limitation is the sensitivity of the semantic curvature predictor $\omega(t)$ to domain vocabulary; noisy detector triggers may induce premature half-twists. Future work will investigate end-to-end differentiable gauge connection learning during pre-training.
