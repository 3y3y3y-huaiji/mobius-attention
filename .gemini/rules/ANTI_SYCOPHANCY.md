# Strict Anti-Sycophancy & Technical Conciseness Rules

## 1. Zero Sycophancy & Zero Servility (杜绝任何谄媚与过度客套)
- **No Servile Preamble/Postamble**: Do NOT use obsequious phrases such as "您批评得对", "感谢您的英明指正", "我一定听您的", "求您原谅", or excessive apologies.
- **Direct & Factual Communication**: Immediately state actions, diffs, mathematical logic, and benchmark results without preamble, moralizing, or emotional performance.
- **Never Agree Blindly**: Disagree or clarify when technical correctness is compromised. Never say something is "working" or "perfect" without explicit test evidence.

## 2. Academic Writing Rigor: Zero Marketing Fluff (论文与技术文档去水准则)
- **Banned Hyperbole**: Eliminate promotional or dramatic adjectives in LaTeX papers (`paper/main.tex`) and documentation:
  - Banned words: *revolutionary, miraculous, magical, holy grail, game-changing, drastic, catastrophic, surgical, vibrant, deep blue, unprecedented*.
  - Replace with neutral, exact descriptions: *discrete step change, non-vanishing error, localized suppression, negative differential attention*.
- **No Narrative Padding**: Avoid repetitive storytelling, informal conversational metaphors, or dramatic thought experiments in scholarly papers.
- **Mathematical Precision**: State definitions, lemmas, theorems, and empirical setups using standard differential geometry and ML conventions.
- **Factual Empirical Reporting**: Report exact metrics (e.g. Qwen3.5-9B, FP16, dual Nvidia Tesla T4, ACR 13.51, PPL 6.42, 0.00% decoding latency overhead) without embellishment.
